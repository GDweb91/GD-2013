A Website That Works as Hard as Your Volunteers

Church website design and nonprofit web design for Boston and Greater Massachusetts

Need a new website for your church or nonprofit in Boston? GD Pro Web Designs builds affordable WordPress websites for churches, ministries and community nonprofits across Greater Boston. Each site includes online giving, an events calendar, sermon or video pages, and bilingual English/Spanish options. Registered nonprofits get reduced rates, and you work directly with the designer.

Most church and nonprofit sites we see have the same problems. They were built years ago by a volunteer who has since moved on. Only one person knows the password. The service times are out of date, and the site is hard to use on a phone, which is where most first-time visitors find you. We fix that by building a clean, fast site your team can update in minutes, and by putting the things people look for first (when, where, how to give, how to help) right on the home page.

Giving back matters to us. GD Pro Web Designs is a small freelance studio, so we give through our skills: web design, graphic design and SEO at reduced rates for organizations that serve the community, and in some special cases at no cost.
