# Pitch Email — AI Visibility Snapshot (Existing Clients)

How to use: copy the body below per client, fill in the bracketed fields, send from your normal address. Keep it 1-to-1 (not a bulk blast) — the whole pitch relies on it reading as personal, not a mail-merge campaign.

Fields to fill per client:
- `[Name]` — contact's first name
- `[Client Business]` — their business name
- `[service]` — the specific service category to reference (use whatever they're known for — "plumber," "restaurant," "dentist," etc.)
- `[City]` — their town

---

## Subject line (pick one)

1. Quick question: does ChatGPT know [Client Business] exists?
2. I tested what AI says about [Client Business] — wanted to share
3. Free 5-minute AI visibility check for [Client Business]

## Email body

Hi [Name],

Quick one — I've been testing how local businesses show up when people ask AI tools (ChatGPT, Gemini, Google's AI answers) for recommendations, instead of just typing into Google the old way.

I ran the test on my own business first. The good news: when someone asks specifically for a "[service] in Everett MA," I show up first. The surprising part: when someone asks for the *best* [service] in the area — a slightly different way people commonly phrase it — I didn't show up at all. Six other companies did, including one that isn't even local to Massachusetts.

Turns out AI tools answer "best of" questions very differently than a direct search, and it comes down to a few specific, fixable things (mostly online reviews, and a couple of technical details on the site) — not a mystery, just something almost nobody checks for yet.

I'd like to run the same 5-minute test on [Client Business] and send you a short, plain-English report — free, no strings — showing exactly what AI tools say (or don't say) about you right now, and who's showing up in your place.

Want me to run it? Just reply "yes" and I'll have it back to you within a couple of days.

Talk soon,
JA
GD Pro Web Designs
(617) 771-0645

## Notes

- Leads with **your own real gap**, not a hypothetical — this is what makes it credible instead of salesy. Don't sanitize it into "everything's great, but you could be even better."
- The free offer is the hook; the report itself (built from `ai-visibility-audit-template.md`) is what opens the door to the paid fix. Don't mention pricing in this email — that's a follow-up conversation once they've seen their own gap.
- If a client replies "yes," the workflow is: run the query bank from the audit template → fill in the Client Report section → send as the follow-up email or a short PDF.
