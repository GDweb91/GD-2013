Local SEO For Small Businesses

Show Up in Google Maps, Bing &
Apple Business Search in Jamaica Plain

Do you need more clients for your small business? More visits to your website? More sales?

There are thousands of searches for local businesses every day in every city of Massachusetts. Most calls to a local restaurant or auto repair shop come from an online search made on a desktop computer or a mobile device. Print and radio advertising are better than nothing, but in today's world it's essential that local businesses be visible on the web — starting with a properly optimized Google Maps listing.

Ways to Get Your Jamaica Plain Business Found in Local Search

  • Paid advertising & organic search placements
  • Improve your Google, Bing & Apple Business Profiles
  • Social media integration — Facebook, LinkedIn, Pinterest, Instagram
  • Generate more client reviews
  • Reputable business directory listings, done carefully
  • Backlink creation through article submission & blogging
  • Google Ads, Bing PPC & Yahoo PPC management
  • Social media advertising — Facebook, YouTube, Pinterest, Instagram, LinkedIn
  • Review site presence, like Yelp
  • Local deals, like Groupon

Paid ads put your business at the top of search results fast, but they can get expensive if not properly managed. A well-optimized Google, Bing, or Apple Business profile is more affordable and takes longer to build — but the results last. And a word of caution: listing your business on every single online directory can hurt your site more than it helps — it takes a deliberate strategy, not volume.
