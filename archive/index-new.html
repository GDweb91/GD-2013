Migration Service

Migrate Your Website to WordPress
Without Losing Your SEO Rankings

Worried that migrating to WordPress will destroy your Google rankings? It doesn't have to. Our SEO-safe WordPress migration process protects your existing traffic while giving you a modern, manageable platform.

01
Full SEO Audit

We document all your existing URLs, rankings, meta data, backlinks, and traffic before touching anything.

02
301 Redirect Map

Every old URL gets a proper 301 redirect to its new WordPress equivalent — Google follows them and preserves your authority.

03
On-Page SEO Transfer

All title tags, meta descriptions, header structure, schema markup, and internal links are rebuilt and improved in WordPress.

04
Post-Migration Monitoring

We monitor Google Search Console and Analytics for 30–60 days after launch to catch and fix any ranking fluctuations immediately.
