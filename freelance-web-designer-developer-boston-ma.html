Our Web Design & Development Process

Every company will use a different process, but this will give you a general idea of how we approach the web design and development process.

Our Web Design & Development Services

Gather Information About Your Business

Probably the most complicated and at the same time the most important part of the process of creating a new website is gathering the necessary information, that will help us understand your company and its goals in order to design a susccessful website.

Defining your target audience is vital to give us a clear understanding of the look and feel of your website, as well as appropiately developing your site with the SEO best practices, so your future clients can find you easly on Google Search Results.

Information We Need To Gather:
  • Your business logo and images to be used. Usually we require it to be in a webp, jpg, png or psd format.

    • If you don't have a business logo, or it is outdated, we can redesign or design a new logotype for your company.**

  • Your copy or website content If you don't have written content to describe your services and/or products, we can either write it for you ( we hire professional SEO content writer) or you hire your own content writer or even write it yourself (Which we don't really recommend since, your content plays a very important role in your website rankings on search engine results). We strongly advice hiring a content writer, since they understand SEO best practices and write content accordingly.
  • Main Purpose of your website, company goals, target audience
Plan and wireframe your website

After we gathered enough information:

  • Product or services pictures
  • Business logo
  • Content (Copy or description of your products or services.)
  • Business Goals and Website Porpuse,
We Plan and wireframe your website and show it to you for your approval.

This will help and guide us to design your website arund your content and user interaction. It will also help us to determine the kind of technology to use to develop your website.

Some times a static website developed with HTML5, CSS and javascript would be enough and more affordable, but in other situations a more complex technology is needed, so we would probably suggest the use of WORDPRESS, SHOPIFY, WOOCOMMERCE (if you need to accept online payments) ( a Content Management System) which will let you do your own updates and add more pages and content easly without the need of hiring a webmaster or web developer.

If you decide to use a Content Management System, we can show you affordable ways to build your website. Or design and develop a custom Wordpress website.

GDWebPros will design and develop of a responsive website for your company, which is a recomendation from Google for any business to provide their customers with a more user friendly experience on mobile devices.

Web Design / UI Best Practices

As skilled UI designers we will start the design process of your website according to the information gathered, your company goals and your customers.

We must probably email you with the mock-ups of your web site for your approval. It is important that you remember that the more time you take in your revision of the mock-up the longer will be the process to finish the design face of your website. After you have approved the design we can start the Web Development process.

Web Development or Production process

It's time to code your website. Whether you decided to have an interactive and responsive website or a static open.

We, then will use a content management system (CMS) like Wordpress, Joomla, Etc. or design and develop a static website with HTML5, CSS and Javascript.

This is the part where we put together the designed parts, making what used to be an image (your website design) into an interactive or static website, containing image sliders, your company colors and logo, your content or copy.

We integrate into every website we design, web mail forms to gather information from your clients, and interactive maps that will help your clients get directions to your business location using Google Maps technology.

We will also, at this stage, run a series of tests to make sure your website is not only mobile friendly, but also web standards compliant.
