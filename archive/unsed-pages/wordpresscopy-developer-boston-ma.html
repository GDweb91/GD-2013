What is Wordpress?

Wordpress and Joomla are just two free open source tools to create interactive and Responsive websites for your small business.

Both platforms are based on PHP and MYSQL.

A great advantage of using WordPress Content Management System is:

  • Easy to use interface for non technical users.
  • It lets you update your content or restaurant menu easy!
  • Add pages or update your blog.
  • Creating links is a breeze.

    • and at the same time...

  • Making it SEO friendly (easy to optimize it for the search engines with plugins) and very important,
  • Most Wordpress themes are responsive or mobile friendly.

Wordpress is a great solution for your small business website, even if you become a big company later and you need to add a shopping cart o more sophisticated interactivity to your site.

We have used WordPress to create websites for restaurants, non profits, churches, construction companies and other start up businesses.
