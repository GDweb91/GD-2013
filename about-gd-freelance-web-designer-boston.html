Our Story

Meet JA Armira —
Your Boston-Area Freelance Web Designer

GD Pro Web Designs was founded in 2005 with a simple but powerful mission: give small businesses in Boston and Greater Massachusetts the same quality online presence that large companies enjoy — at a price they can actually afford.

The business was born from a personal experience: noticing how many local shops, restaurants, and service providers in the Greater Boston area were practically invisible on Google while their competitors — often with inferior products — ranked on the first page simply because they had a better website and an SEO strategy.

Over the past 20 years, GD Pro Web Designs has helped construction firms, restaurants, law offices, tax services, non-profits, medical practices, and transportation companies across 40+ Massachusetts cities grow their online presence. Every project is handled personally — no outsourcing, no account managers, no agency markup.

  • Custom WordPress themes built from scratch — no page-builder bloat
  • SEO strategies that target real buyers in your city and industry
  • Google Ads campaigns optimized for local lead generation
  • Graphic design: logos, menus, brochures, signage & more
  • Fully bilingual service — English & Spanish (Hablamos Español)
  • Direct access to your designer throughout every project
