Why It Matters

Why Boston Restaurants Are Switching to Digital Menu Boards

Paper menus and chalkboards are falling behind. Digital displays boost sales, reduce printing costs, and give your restaurant a modern edge — all from a single 55" TV.

Increase Average Order Value

Highlighted specials and combo offers on screen have been shown to lift average ticket sizes by 10–30% compared to static menus.

Eliminate Printing Costs

Stop paying to reprint menus every time prices change. Update your digital board in minutes — no design fees, no delays.

Promote Specials Instantly

Run happy hour promos, daily specials, and seasonal offers with a time-based schedule. The right message displays automatically at the right time.

Look More Professional

A sleek digital menu board signals quality to new customers before they even order — critical for first impressions in competitive Boston neighborhoods.

Schedule Menus by Time

Automatically switch from a breakfast menu to lunch to dinner based on time of day — no manual switching, no staff effort.

Managed Remotely

We update your content from our office in Everett, MA. No technician visits, no downtime — just a quick remote update and you're live.

The Process

How We Set Up Your Restaurant Display

From our first call to your TV going live — most installs are completed within 5–7 business days.

1

Free Consultation & Menu Audit

We review your current menu, branding, and restaurant layout. We'll recommend the right screen size, number of zones, and content schedule for your space.

2

Custom Design — Menu Board & Promo Slides

Our team designs a branded 1920×1080 menu board layout using your logo, colors, and full menu. We also design promotional slides for specials and combo offers.

3

Hardware Setup & Configuration

We configure a Raspberry Pi 4 with your content preloaded, set up the signage platform (Yodeck), and schedule your content rotations before we even arrive at your location.

4

On-Site Installation

We come to your restaurant, connect the hardware to your TV and WiFi, test everything live, and walk your staff through how it works. Done in a few hours.

5

Ongoing Weekly Updates

Each week, simply text or email us your new specials. We update the display remotely — no visit required. Changes go live within 24 hours, guaranteed.
