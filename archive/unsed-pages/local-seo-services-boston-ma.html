What is Local Internet Marketing?

If you are a local business (landscaping, construction company, pizzeria, restaurant or any other local business in Everett, Malden, Chelsea, Revere, Medford or any other city in Massachusetts), your main stream of business comes from nearby clients in your city and the cities near you.

Then, you should make sure that your local business address, phone number, website address, front images of your business, products and services, and other important information about your local business are listed on Google My Business Profile (Previously: Google Maps) Apple business Maps, Yahoo and Bing Maps.

If somebody in your local area is searching for your services or products, your business should be listed on Google, Yahoo and Bing maps.

Google will prioritize local businesses on searches related to specific cities and locations. So focusing on improving local search results will result in more visits to your business.

Google Local SEO Ranking Factors

  • Relevance: Which basically refers to how well your local business listing matches a users' search.
  • Distance: Which refers to how close your business is located from the location the search is being made.
  • Prominence: This has more to do with how well your business is known around your city.

But Google will also rank your local business for how well your Google Business Profile defines your services.

  • Also you need to make sure your business is connected to Apple Business Connect in order to show in Apple Map locations.
  • Optimization for voice search.
  • Another important factor in your local rankings is the number of business reviews you have. Your review score also very important and your interaction with your clients reviews.
  • Ensure your business name, phone number and address are consistent.
