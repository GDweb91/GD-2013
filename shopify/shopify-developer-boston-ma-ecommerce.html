Platform Guide

Shopify vs WooCommerce —
Which Is Right for You?

Both are excellent e-commerce platforms — the right choice depends on your budget, technical comfort level, and how you want to run your store day-to-day. We build both and we'll give you an honest recommendation for your specific situation.

Shopify is better if…
  • You want a hosted, low-maintenance solution
  • Reliability and uptime are critical
  • You prefer predictable monthly costs
  • You're selling physical products
  • You want built-in payments (Shopify Payments)
WooCommerce is better if…
  • You want maximum flexibility and control
  • You already have a WordPress site
  • You want to avoid platform transaction fees
  • You need complex custom functionality
  • Lower cost at high sales volume

Not sure which to choose? Contact us for a free consultation — we'll walk through your specific needs and give a straight recommendation.
