What is Wordpress?

Wordpress and Joomla are two free open source web development platforms to create interactive and responsive websites for any busines, small or large.

Both platforms are based on PHP and MYSQL.

A great advantage of using WordPress Content Management System (CMS) is:

  • Easy to use interface for non technical users.
  • It lets you update your content (picture, text/copy) or restaurant menu easy!
  • Add pages or write new articles in your blog.
  • Creating links is a breeze.

    • and at the same time...

  • Making it SEO friendly (easy to optimize it for the search engines with plugins) and more importantly,
  • most Wordpress Themes are responsive or mobile friendly right out of the box!

Wordpress is a great solution to create your small business website, even if you become a big company later or you need to add a shopping cart or online payments o more sophisticated interactivity to your site.
