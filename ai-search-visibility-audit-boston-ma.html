AI search visibility (sometimes called GEO or AEO — generative/answer engine optimization) is whether AI tools like ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, and Google's AI Overview mention your business when someone asks for a recommendation, instead of a competitor or a directory listing.

Traditional SEO ranks your page in a list of links. AI search visibility is about whether an AI assistant actually names your business in the answer it gives someone, which depends more heavily on structured data, review signals, and clearly extractable answer-shaped content — not just keywords and backlinks.

We run the real questions your customers would ask — for example "best plumber in Everett MA" — through multiple AI platforms and record exactly what comes back: whether you're named, what's said about you, and who shows up instead.

A short, plain-English report showing the exact questions we tested, whether your business appeared in each AI answer, which competitors appeared instead, and the most likely reason for any gap found. Request yours free.

Simple fixes like adding missing structured data can be picked up within weeks. Trust-signal fixes like building review volume take longer, but tend to have the biggest impact on whether AI tools name you for competitive, "best of" style questions.

No one can guarantee exactly what an AI model will say — the same is true of any SEO or marketing service. What we guarantee is a clear, honest picture of where you stand today and a concrete, prioritized plan to improve it.
